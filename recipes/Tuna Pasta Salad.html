Tuna Pasta Salad

Tuna pasta salad is an American culinary evolution that combined late-19th-century mayonnaise-based tuna salads with budget-friendly mid-20th-century pasta and canned provisions.

ingredients are
  • Short pasta (such as macaroni, shells, or rotini)
  • Canned tuna (drained and flaked)
  • Celery (chopped)
  • or sweet onion (finely diced)
  • Peas or sweetcorn (thawed or drained)
  • (or plain Greek yogurt)
  • Vinegar or fresh lemon juice
  • Dijon mustard
  • and black pepper

How to prepare it

  1. Cook the pasta: Boil your chosen pasta in salted water until al dente, drain it, and rinse it under cold water to cool completely.
  2. Mix the dressing: Whisk together the mayonnaise, vinegar (or lemon juice), Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl.
  3. Combine everything: Toss the cooled pasta, flaked tuna, celery, onion, and peas together in a large mixing bowl.
  4. and chill: Pour the dressing over the ingredients, stir gently until everything is evenly coated, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
